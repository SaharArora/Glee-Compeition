# Research process lessons

- Keep competition optimization and frozen research arms in separate branches, worktrees,
  registries, and completion criteria.
- Chat summaries are not resumable state. Record process commands, artifact hashes, branch,
  commit, authorization, and exact remaining gaps before relying on a long computation.
- A quiet heavy process is neither progress evidence nor failure. Use bounded commands, atomic
  artifacts, and small completion certificates; never restart solely because context compacted.
- Persistence follows a route's completion condition. A verifier-backed negative result can
  close a route; it does not authorize endless reformulation.
- Separate route state from evidence maturity. Passing tests or finishing a fit is not the same
  as an isolated verifier-backed claim.
- Start with the cheapest assumption-killing test and keep early mechanism routes independent.
  Cross-pollination begins only after the root reconciles a bounded wave.
- Preserve failed candidates and audits by exact version. A new seed, prompt wording, threshold,
  or cosmetic refactor cannot erase the old failure or create material novelty.
- Four wrappers being mutually identical is weaker than treatment-off parity: they can share the
  same forbidden mechanism. Always compare them with an independently specified off-core.
- Reusing the same scenario is weaker than treatment isolation. Stateful agent RNG, diagnostic
  callbacks, eligibility, and support selection need named immutable surfaces or a paired payoff
  can be contaminated while looking perfectly matched.
- Delivery is weaker than observability, and observability is weaker than causal responsiveness.
  Test the receiver policy with a text-only perturbation before designing a language payoff run.
