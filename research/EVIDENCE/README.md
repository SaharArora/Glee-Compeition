# Evidence records

Small durable certificates and hashes for large external artifacts live here. Large logs may
remain outside git, but every claim must link an immutable summary, reproduction command, and
input/output hashes.

- `WAVE5D_COMPETITION_PREP.json` reconstructs the Jordan-versus-Factorial00 trace from the exact
  already-exposed Wave 5B ledger and freezes two unimplemented Jordan-v2 hypotheses. It is
  mechanism-generation evidence only, never untouched confirmation or promotion evidence.
