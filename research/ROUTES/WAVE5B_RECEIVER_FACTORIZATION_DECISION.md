## Receiver decision

No project evidence supports a named hosted model or local artifact. The admissible choice remains
one independently selected, pre-existing text-responsive receiver with immutable identity:

- hosted: exact provider/model/version, frozen prompts/decoding, instrumented field consumption,
  exact cached replay and spend authorization; or
- local: model/tokenizer/inference/runtime/hardware hashes and deterministic replay under the same
  capability certificate.

The user must supply the 13 inputs in `WAVE5A_RECEIVER_DECISION.md`. Selection must be
treatment-blind and cannot use the four templates, Factorial01/11 payoff, or capability strength to
rank multiple receivers. A generic certificate proves responsiveness/plumbing only.

## Factorization decision

- default for a hosted or nondeterministic receiver: **Design A**, 300 economic strata × two roles
  × two receiver replicates per family;
- default for a byte-deterministic local receiver: **Design B**, 600 strata × two roles, because a
  second identical receiver replicate wastes economic breadth;
- Design C (200 × two roles × three replicates) is reserved for an independently justified highly
  variable receiver.

No design is selected until receiver identity/determinism is frozen. Then freeze the exact 3,600
row root, both separate production pins, and obtain a fresh hostile audit before any outcome.

change to this factorial baseline. Replacing the current baseline with A+C requires the user's

complete refreeze of parity, manifests, hashes and both pins.
