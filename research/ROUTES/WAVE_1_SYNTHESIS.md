## Root challenge to the favored route

R2 has the strongest positive result, but it is only a validity result for one fixed null and
event definition. It does **not** show that concessions above one half predict a profitable
economic response, that a myopic state can update, or that the e-process treatment can be
isolated from the baseline and RNG. It may therefore end as a valid but payoff-irrelevant
process. No acting implementation or favorable crossing result can bypass that distinction.

## Duplicated mechanisms

R1 and the historical-score portion of R2 expose the same common contamination—heuristic
`E_*` values are currently part of the economic control path—so they must not be counted as
two independent failures of the economic theory core. R3 and R4 both concern evaluation
feasibility, but are distinct terminal boundaries: R3 shows the receiver is text-blind; R4
shows the runner cannot certify isolation even if a text-responsive receiver later exists.
